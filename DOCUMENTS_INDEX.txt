ESCALATION PATHS (When Things Go Wrong)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Level 1: Minor Issue (1 person affected)
  Channel: #sprint-podkapot thread
  Escalation: None needed
  SLA: Same day fix
  Reference: RISK_REGISTER.md

Level 2: Blocker (2+ people affected)
  Channel: @backend-lead or @frontend-lead in #sprint-podkapot
  Escalation: 15-min sync (now)
  SLA: 2 hours fix
  Reference: RISK_REGISTER.md

Level 3: Critical (project at risk)
  Channel: #critical (all hands)
  Escalation: Emergency standup (now)
  SLA: 30 min decision
  Reference: RISK_REGISTER.md

Examples:
  "Parser success <80%" → Level 3 (critical)
  "E2E tests flaky >20%" → Level 2 (blocker)
  "CSS alignment off" → Level 1 (minor)
